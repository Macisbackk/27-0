# Super League era coverage, 1996–2026 (October 2026)

Owner's question: are there gaps in years or players from the start of Super League?
This report checks every club that actually played each Super League season against what the game has, then
lists what was filled and what is still missing. Generated by `bun scripts/era-coverage-md.ts`; the per-season
numbers come from `scripts/era-coverage-report.ts`.

## How to read it

- **Club list per season**: the final league tables from the Wikipedia `<year> Super League season` pages (already in
  `data/era-rating-anchors.json`), plus Toronto Wolfpack 2020 (withdrew after six rounds, results expunged) and the
  14 clubs of 2026. That is 388 club-seasons, 374 of them 1996–2025.
- **Spin**: the club-season is an Era Classic spin pool (2026: a Classic Current pool).
- **Own**: recruitable cards pinned to that exact club and season.
- **13 / 17**: those own-season cards cover a full XIII by position (and 17 cards with a full XIII).
- **Notable**: Man of Steel, Dream Team, Lance Todd and Harry Sunderland winners for that club that season
  (Wikipedia, via `data/era-rating-anchors.json`) with a card for that exact club-season.

## What was wrong

1. **Era Classic only spun 67 of the 374 historic club-seasons.** Spin pools come from verified XIIIs
   (`era-wikipedia-squads.json`, `sl-era-verified-squads.json`), and most seasons had none. Most did have a verified
   starting 17 (`era-starting-17s.json`), but no spin XIII and few season cards.
2. **Most club-seasons had no card for that season.** Only 121 club-seasons had 13+ own-season cards. Era sides filled
   the gaps with the player's nearest other season.
3. **`super-league-club-years.json` was wrong.** It listed Hull KR 1999–2006 and 2017, Wakefield 1996–98, Huddersfield
   1996–97 and 2002, Hull FC 1996–97, Salford 1996, 2003 and 2008, Castleford 2005 and 2007, London 2018 and 2020, Leigh
   2018 and 2022, Bradford and York 2025 as Super League seasons. It missed Hull KR 2015–16, Widnes 2014–16, Leigh 2005
   and 2021, London 2014 and 2024, and Salford 2025. Former clubs (Halifax, Sheffield, Oldham, Paris, Workington,
   Gateshead, Crusaders, Toronto) were not in it at all. A London Broncos 2018 (Championship) pool was spinnable.
4. **Former Super League clubs could not be spun.** Their cards were displayed as a current club (Sheffield → Leeds,
   Halifax → Bradford, Paris → Catalans ...).

## What changed

- `data/super-league-club-years.json`: the real seasons for all 25 clubs (Wikipedia season tables).
- `src/lib/clubs/super-league-display.ts`, `src/lib/players/era-teams.ts`: Halifax Panthers, Sheffield Eagles, Oldham RLFC,
  Paris Saint-Germain RL, Workington Town, Gateshead Thunder, Crusaders RL and Toronto Wolfpack are era-playable
  (historic-only) clubs, gated to their Super League seasons.
- `scripts/fill-era-coverage-2026-10.ts` (re-runnable): for every club-season, a spin XIII from its verified starting 17
  (jersey 1–13) when it had none that worked, and a season card for every player in the 17 and every award winner.
  A new card copies the same player's nearest card (name, nationality, date of birth); position is the jersey he wore
  in that 17; rating is his nearest card's rating moved along the age curve, never above his best card. Then
  `scripts/build-era-ratings.ts` (honours-anchored peaks, season curve, club-finish layer) re-rated all era cards, so
  the new cards sit on the same scale (Man of Steel seasons at the player's peak, Dream Team seasons at peak − 1 ...).
- Club-seasons with no verified 17 got one from a real match line-up (`data/era-coverage-lineups-2026-10.json`, one
  Rugby League Project match page or Wikipedia final page per side). Players with no card anywhere got a fresh card
  only with a cited bio (`data/era-coverage-new-players-2026-10.json`: nationality, date of birth); they start at 82
  (81 off the bench), the squad-player level, and the club-finish layer moves the side as a whole.
- Widnes Vikings 2003, Widnes Vikings 2004, Halifax Panthers 2003: no single line-up resolves to 13 different cards (Halifax 2003: one player
  unverifiable; Widnes 2003–04: two different Julian O'Neills in the 17), so the XIII is that season's cards at the
  positions they played in the sourced 17s.
- `data/player-name-aliases.ts`: Andrew/Andy Farrell, Matty/Mat Ashurst, Robert/Rob Butler.
- `src/lib/players/era-starting-17s.ts`: a starting 17 may spell positions out ("FULLBACK"), as Sheffield 1998 does.
- `st-helens-hist-jason-hooper*`: loose forward (was stand-off), per the 2006 Grand Final line-up.
- `gateshead-hist-martin-masella` (1998): filed under Gateshead, who were not in Super League until 1999; he played for
  Leeds in 1998, so the card is Leeds Rhinos 1998.

## Calibration after the fill

More club-seasons and own-season cards change what a typical Era Classic draft looks like, so the 27-0 formula
anchors were re-measured (`RUNS=400 MODES=era bun scripts/sim-era-classic-perfect-season.ts`):

| Era draft tier | Avg rating before → after | Chemistry before → after | 27-0 % with old anchors (after data) |
|---|---|---|---|
| weak | 84.71 → 83.78 | 0.61 → 0.24 | 0 |
| random (typical) | 86.64 → 85.60 | 0.63 → 0.27 | 0.38 (was 3.1 on main) |
| good | 87.06 → 86.24 | 0.65 → 0.27 | 0.92 (was 5.7) |
| best (near-perfect) | 88.50 → 87.50 | 0.66 → 0.27 | 7.6 (was 31.0) |

Typical drafts are ~1 point lower (pools now hold that season's card, often below the player's peak card) and
chemistry is lower (fewer shared card years). `SIM_FORMULA_MODE_SCALE.era` is now typical 85.6 / elite 87.5 /
chemistry 0.3 (was 86.6 / 88.5 / 0.6), which puts the same draft quality back on the same odds.
`GAUNTLET_FIELD_OFFSET` 13 → 21: the champion sides now field their own-season cards and sit higher on the Era ladder;
21 restores the near-perfect clean-sweep rate (Classic 21.6%, Era 30.0% vs 22.0% / 31.8% on main).
One-Club XI ladders (`one-club-scale.ts`) were regenerated: 18 clubs now (Crusaders, Halifax, Leigh, Sheffield and
Widnes added), near-perfect 27-0 26.8–31.1% per club.
