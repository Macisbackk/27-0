# NRL ratings audit

Generated by `scripts/nrl/build-nrl-ratings.ts` (re-run it to refresh this file). Standalone: nothing in the game reads these ratings yet.

## Scales

* **Season (era) cards 1998–2025: 80–99, minimum 80** — the same scale as the Super League era cards (`data/era-season-ratings.json`). File: `data/nrl/nrl-ratings.json` (one row per player-season with the reason).
* **Current 2026 cards: 70–99** — fringe, young and yet-to-debut players can sit in the 70s. File: `data/nrl/nrl-ratings-2026.json` (every player in `nrl-current-squads-2026.json`).

## Formula (mirrors `scripts/build-era-ratings.ts`)

1. **Peak** per player = 82 + games (1998+ NRL games, +1 per 40, max +5) + scoring (backs +1/+1.5/+2 at 0.30/0.42/0.60 tries a game; forwards and unknown positions +0.5/+1 at 0.25/0.45 — props, hookers and back-rowers are credited through games and selection instead) + representative level (Origin or Australia/New Zealand Test +4, both +5; Tonga/Samoa/Fiji/PNG/England Test +2).
   * Honours **floors**: Dally M Medals + Golden Boots ×1 97, ×2 98, ×3+ 99 (+1 with the Hall of Fame or two Clive Churchill / Wally Lewis medals); Immortal 99; Hall of Fame 96; Dally M Team of the Year ×1 93, ×2 94, ×3 95, ×4+ 96; Origin / Australia–NZ Test 89; other Test nations 87.
   * Honours **caps** (no-one outrates the award winners on games alone): no representative football 89 (under 50 NRL games 87); Pacific/England Test 91; Origin / Australia–NZ Test 93; Team of the Year ×1 95, ×2 96, ×3+ 97; Hall of Fame 98; Medal / Golden Boot 99. Each of the first four gets +1 for a Clive Churchill or Wally Lewis Medal.
2. **Season arc**: Dally M Medal / Golden Boot season = peak (−2 at 21 or under, −1 a year past 32); Team of the Year season = peak − 1 (same age penalty); every other season ≤ peak − age curve (19: −7, 20: −5, 21: −4, 22: −2, 23: −1, 24–32: 0, 33: −1 … 36+: −4) − years from his nearest award season (max 3) − part season (1–2 games −5, 3–7 −3, 8–12 −1). A prime-age (23–31) season of 10+ games within two years of a Team of the Year season is at least peak − 2 − 2×distance; a Clive Churchill / Wally Lewis season at least peak − 2; a premiership season with 15+ games +1 (within his cap).
   * Age comes from `nrl-card-info.json` (date of birth, birth year, or the middle of a birth-year range from a dated age). With none, a player whose first NRL season is after 1998 is taken to be 20 that season, and a Hall of Famer 19 in the first season of his career span (both marked `age est.` in the reason); other 1998 starters with no birth date get no age adjustment.
3. **Club finish**: each club-season's best-13 average against its final ladder position (`nrl-ladders.json`, premiers count as 2nd or better). A least-squares line of rating vs finish is fitted; top-half sides more than a point below the line come up and bottom-half sides more than a point above it come down, by 0.6 × the gap (max 3). Award seasons are left alone.
4. **2026 current**: the 2026 season on the wider scale — part season −8/−5/−2, 2026 ladder ±1 for regulars (top 4 +1, bottom 3 −1), then 2 below the era scale (1 for a 2025 Team of the Year player; the 2026 Dally M Medallist keeps his rating), as Super League current cards sit below its era cards. 2026 Dally M Medal and 2026 Origin debuts count. Squad members with no 2026 game: last season card − 2 a year away; no NRL games: 72 (Top 30) or 70 (development list).

Decision: the NRL is the stronger league, so an established NRL first-grader (10+ games in a season) averages about 87 on the era scale — 1–1.5 above the average Super League era card — while the whole NRL database (every fringe player AFL Tables lists) averages the same as Super League's. The very best land 97–99 (Johns, Thurston, Smith, Slater, Cronk, Lockyer, Cleary, Tedesco).

Club seasons vs ladder: correlation of best-13 rating with finish r = -0.53 before the club step, -0.66 after (447 club-seasons; 103 sides moved; negative = higher-rated sides finish higher).

## Things that look off / known limits

* 282 players (all 1998 starters without a sourced birth date) get no age adjustment, so their late-career seasons are not lowered.
* 1403 players have an estimated age (20 in their first NRL season). A late starter (e.g. a 25-year-old debutant) is treated as younger, so his early seasons are rated a little low.
* Origin and Test selections are known by debut year (and the 2017/2021/2025 tournament squads), not season by season, so representative credit lifts a player's whole career peak rather than the seasons he was picked.
* Positions are sourced for about a third of priority players; the rest are rated with the forward/unknown scoring credit (at most +1), so try-scoring wingers without a sourced position can be a point low.
* Pre-1998 games are not counted, so players who debuted before 1998 (Fittler, Langer, Daley, Ettingshausen …) get less longevity credit; honours floors cover the stars.
* 147 season cards are for players who changed club mid-season; the card uses the first club for the club-finish step.
* 2026 Top-30 lists were found for 11 clubs (Top-30 web lists for 9, plus Rugby League Project pages for Cronulla and the Warriors); Dolphins, Melbourne, Newcastle, North Queensland, Parramatta and South Sydney list only players who played a 2026 game.
* High cards from very few games (≤3 games, ≥92) — honours-driven, check: Andrew Johns 2004 (3 g, 93).
